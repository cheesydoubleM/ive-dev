WHY IVE?

Several tasks at once. Not one tab at a time.

Give each task its own session and model. Start the next one while the first still runs.

One board. Who runs, who waits, who’s done.

When a session needs you it is marked, and you answer it from the board.

Every change comes back to you.

Working and staged diffs for each session, file by file, inside the app. Stage what you accept, annotate what you don’t.

Your machine. Your accounts.

Runs on your computer with the CLI logins you already have. No IVE account. Your CLI talks to its provider as before.

Inside IVE.

From your terminal to the passing check.

IVE for iPhone

Your terminal, everywhere.

Pair the iPhone app with IVE on your Mac. Every session, every terminal and every question an agent asks is then on your phone, over a secure connection, wherever you are. The agents keep running on the Mac.
